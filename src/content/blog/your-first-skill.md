Most of us have a way of doing one part of the job really well. And it lives only in our head.

AI coding agents can now load small instruction files called skills. A skill tells the agent how to do one kind of job, and it loads only when that job comes up.

So write yours down.
How you want a bug report written.
How you review a change before it ships.
How you check a screen before calling it finished.

Then the agent does it your way without you repeating it. Even on days when you forget.

Pick one thing you do well. Write the steps on one page. That's your first skill.
