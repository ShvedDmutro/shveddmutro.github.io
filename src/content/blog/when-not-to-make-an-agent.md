---
title: "When not to make an agent"
description: "Make one for repeated jobs with their own rules. Skip it for one-off tasks."
date: 2026-11-03
tags: ["Agents", "AI coding"]
draft: true
---
Not every task deserves a custom agent.

Make one when:
- you do the same kind of job again and again
- the job needs its own rules that the main agent keeps forgetting
- it should be limited, like read-only

Skip it when:
- you'd use it once
- the job needs everything from the current conversation
- one short instruction in chat does the same thing

A folder full of agents nobody calls is just more to maintain.

Start with one. Add the second when you miss it.
