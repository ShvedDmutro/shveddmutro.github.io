I've worked through three big waves in software.

2009: websites built by hand, one browser bug at a time.
Then React, mobile apps, big backends.
Now AI.

Each time the tools changed completely. The basics didn't.
Clear requirements. Small steps. Tests. Someone who owns the decision, and someone who checks the result.

AI coding agents are the biggest tool change I've seen in 17 years. And they make those basics matter more, not less. An agent does exactly what you wrote, very fast. Give it a vague task, and you get a vague result in ten minutes instead of two days.

The engineers getting the most out of them are the ones who kept the basics.
