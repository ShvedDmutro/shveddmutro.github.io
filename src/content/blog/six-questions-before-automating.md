Before you automate anything with AI, answer these 6 questions.

1. What happens today, step by step? If you can't write it down, AI won't fix it.
2. Which step needs real understanding? That's where AI goes. The rest is normal code.
3. What's the worst thing a wrong answer can do?
4. Who approves the risky steps?
5. How will you know it stopped working?
6. Can the new version run next to the old one for a while?

Most failed automations I've seen skipped question 1.
They automated a process nobody fully understood.
